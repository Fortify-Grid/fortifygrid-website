About Fortify

An AI-optimized, distributed and modular approach to making grids more resilient.

Unlike most electricity infrastructure, batteries are modular, containerized, and relocatable. They can be deployed close to where power is needed, capture changing market opportunities, and be redeployed as the grid’s supply, demand, and locational needs evolve.

Yet bespoke engineering, fragmented procurement, interconnection constraints, revenue uncertainty, and complex tax equity and financing requirements make distributed storage unnecessarily expensive and difficult to scale.

Fortify helps asset owners and utilities deploy distributed battery storage faster and at lower cost. We combine AI-driven intelligence, standardized engineering, intelligent sourcing, and repeatable execution to make projects commercially viable, from small commercial and industrial systems to distribution-level BESS.

Our goal is to improve investment returns and build adaptable storage portfolios that evolve with the grid.
