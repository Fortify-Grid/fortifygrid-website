About

An AI-optimized, distributed and modular approach to making grids more resilient.

We help asset owners and utilities deploy distributed batteries fast, overcoming the cost inefficiencies of small scale and the interconnection, revenue and tax equity and financing barriers to commercially viable battery storage, from small C&I behind-the-meter systems to distribution-level BESS.
