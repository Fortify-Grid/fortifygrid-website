Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

What we collect

When you sign in to FortifyGrid apps (via Google or a one-time email code), we receive your name and email address. We record when you last signed in and the changes administrators make to access. We do not receive your Google password or any other Google data.

How we use it

Only to identify you, decide which FortifyGrid apps you may use, and contact you about your access. We do not sell or share it with third parties, except the service providers that run our sign-in and hosting (Cloudflare, Google).

Website visitors

The public website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Information you send through the contact form is used only to reply to you.

Your choices

You can ask us to remove your account or data at any time.
