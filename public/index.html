About Fortify

An AI-optimized, distributed and modular approach to making grids more resilient.

Unlike most electricity infrastructure, batteries are modular, containerized, and relocatable. They can and should move as grid supply, demand, network topology, and locational needs evolve.

Yet bespoke engineering, fragmented procurement, layered supplier margins, interconnection constraints, revenue uncertainty, and complex tax equity and financing keep distributed storage unnecessarily expensive and difficult to scale.

Fortify helps asset owners and utilities deploy distributed battery storage faster and at lower cost. We combine AI-driven analysis and agentic workflows, rigorous but standardized engineering, intelligent sourcing across global FEOC-compliant supply chains, and relentless process improvement to deliver repeatable, commercially viable small BESS projects, from BTM C&I systems to distribution-level FTM BESS.

At Fortify, we put these principles into practice: making distributed storage simple to deploy, economical to scale, and adaptable by design.
